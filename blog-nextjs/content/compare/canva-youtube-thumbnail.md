## What makes a thumbnail work, in any tool

The tool matters less than three choices you make before you open it. These
apply to a Canva design and to an AI idea alike.

### One idea per thumbnail

Viewers decide fast while they scroll. Show **one** thing: a
face reacting, a place, a before and after, or a number. If you need two
sentences to explain the picture, it holds too much.

### Text that adds, not repeats

The title already sits under the thumbnail. Use the thumbnail text for what
the title does not say: the stake ("7 DAYS"), the feeling ("STUCK"), or a
question. Three to five words is plenty.

### Readable at phone size

Many viewers watch on a phone. Before you upload, shrink the thumbnail
and check the face, the text and the main object. The
[thumbnail preview tool](/tools/youtube-thumbnail-preview) shows it on the
home page, in search and on a phone.

## When switching makes sense

If you design each thumbnail by hand and enjoy it, Canva is a fine home. If
you post often, make thumbnails late at night, or keep guessing which version
would win, try starting from the video instead: describe it, compare three
ideas, change what you want in plain words, and let YouTube Test & Compare
pick the winner. Not sure where your channel stands? The
[free channel audit](/youtube-channel-audit) looks at your recent videos first.

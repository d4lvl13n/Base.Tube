## How to use these ideas

Pick the page closest to your video, not to your channel. A gaming channel can
post a tutorial, a challenge or a reaction, and each of those has its own
layout. Open the page, choose a layout, and change three things: the subject,
the colour accent and the words.

## What every good thumbnail has in common

The looks on this hub are very different, but the thumbnails that work share a
few rules:

- **One focus.** One face, one object or one moment. Everything else supports it.
- **Readable at phone size.** If the idea is not clear in a second when the
  image is small, simplify it.
- **Three words or fewer.** The text adds something the image cannot say; it
  does not repeat the title.
- **A clear accent colour.** One strong colour against a calm background draws
  the eye to the right place.
- **An honest promise.** The thumbnail shows something the video delivers.
  Misleading thumbnails disappoint viewers and can break YouTube's policies.

## Why every example is original

Searches for thumbnail ideas usually lead to galleries of other creators' work,
official game art or photos of famous people. You cannot reuse any of those on
your own channel. Every example on these pages is an original image generated
with Base.Tube: it shows the layout, the colours and the text treatment, so you
can make your own version without copying anyone.

## From idea to thumbnail

Once you have a layout, you can build it in any image editor or describe your
video in the Base.Tube Studio and generate options in that style. Before you
upload, check the result at feed size with the free
[thumbnail preview](/tools/youtube-thumbnail-preview) and make sure it meets the
[YouTube thumbnail size](/youtube-thumbnail-size) rules.

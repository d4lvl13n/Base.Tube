## A Shorts cover is a different object

A regular YouTube thumbnail is a 16:9 image that sells a video before anyone
sees it. A YouTube Shorts thumbnail works differently. In the swipe feed, the
Short starts playing straight away, so nobody sees the cover there. The cover
matters where Shorts appear as tiles: your channel's Shorts tab, search
results and hashtag pages. There, viewers compare several vertical tiles side
by side and pick one.

That changes the design. The frame is 9:16, tall and narrow. The tile is
small. And parts of it can be covered by the title or by buttons, depending on
where it is shown.

## The safe middle band

Put everything important in the middle of the frame: the face, the object and
the words. Leave the top and bottom edges for background. A rule that works
everywhere: if you split the cover into five horizontal strips, keep the text
and the subject inside the middle three.

## How to change a Shorts thumbnail

YouTube offers two routes, and they are not the same.

- **In the YouTube app:** you choose a frame from the Short, before you upload
  or later from your video list (More, then Edit). You can add text and
  filters to that frame.
- **In YouTube Studio on a computer:** you can upload your own image. Open
  Content, choose Shorts, edit the Short and click Upload file under
  Thumbnail. YouTube says this needs a verified account and that changes can
  take time to appear.

If you only use your phone, a simple trick is to start the Short with your
designed cover as a still for a moment, then select that frame.

## Size

YouTube recommends a 9:16 image for Shorts covers, with 2160 × 3840 pixels as
the recommended resolution and a minimum height of 640 pixels. Designing at
1080 × 1920 keeps the same shape with a lighter file. For regular videos, the
[YouTube thumbnail size guide](/youtube-thumbnail-size) has the 16:9 numbers.

## Five layouts for Shorts

- **Before and after:** two stacked halves, the result at the bottom.
- **Face hook:** one face from the chest up, three words across the middle.
- **Result first:** the finished dish, drawing or trick, big and sharp.
- **Series card:** one colour and one number badge, every episode.
- **Question card:** a question the Short answers, with one clue object.

## Text that survives a narrow tile

Two to four words in capitals, in a heavy sans-serif with a thick outline.
Narrow tiles punish long words, so prefer "FIRST TRY" to "UNBELIEVABLE". The
[YouTube thumbnail fonts guide](/youtube-thumbnail-fonts) lists free fonts
that stay readable at small sizes.

## Make the opening match the cover

Because the swipe feed shows the video, not the cover, the first second of the
Short does the job of a thumbnail for most viewers. Open on the same image as
the cover: the same face, the same result, the same words. Viewers who come
from your channel page then get exactly what the tile promised.

## What makes a R.E.P.O. thumbnail work

R.E.P.O. is a co-op horror game where small, wobbly robots carry fragile
valuables through haunted places while monsters hunt them. The best videos
are both funny and scary, and the best thumbnails show both at once: a robot
proud of its vase, and a monster right behind it.

Many viewers see your thumbnail small: on a phone, in the sidebar, on a TV.
Dark horror scenes fall apart at that size and turn into black rectangles.
One strong light, big robots and a clear monster survive. Every rule on this
page follows from that.

## Funny and scary in the same frame

The core image of R.E.P.O. is the contrast between a cute, clumsy semibot and
a terrifying monster. Put both in the frame. The robot is busy with its loot,
the monster is close, and the viewer sees the danger before the robot does.
That small story makes people click to see what happens next.

## Let the eyes do the acting

Semibots have no mouths, but their eyes and their floppy bodies are very
expressive. Wide eyes for panic, squinting eyes for sneaking, a limp body
flying through the air for a funny death. Make the robots large enough that
the eyes read at phone size. If your channel shows your face, a face cam
screaming or laughing next to the game adds the human reaction.

## Five layouts, one per video type

Most R.E.P.O. videos fit one of five shapes:

- **Monster behind:** a robot with loot, a monster right behind it.
- **Loot disaster:** a valuable shattering and the value dropping.
- **Extraction:** the team at the extraction point with the money goal.
- **Face cam:** your reaction next to the scariest or funniest moment.
- **Monster close-up:** one monster huge, for guides and tier lists.

Pick the layout that matches the promise of your video, then change the
monster, the valuable and the words.

## Colours: dark, with one light

The levels are dark, so the thumbnail is mostly navy. Put one strong light on
the subject: warm lamp light for sneaking, red for the monster's eyes, green
and gold for the extraction point and money. A frame that is all dark loses
against brighter thumbnails in the feed.

## Text: a word or a number

Three words is the limit. Short warnings work well ("RUN", "SHHH", "BEHIND
YOU"), and a dollar amount says everything for haul and disaster videos. Use a
heavy rounded font with a thick black outline. See the
[YouTube thumbnail fonts guide](/youtube-thumbnail-fonts) for options.

## A REPO thumbnail background

Use a real level: Headman Manor, McJannek Station or Swiftbroom Academy. Keep
it dark and slightly blurred so the robots and the monster stay in front. More
ideas in the [YouTube thumbnail backgrounds guide](/youtube-thumbnail-backgrounds).

## Size and checks

Upload at 1280 × 720 pixels, in 16:9. For file size and format limits, see the
[YouTube thumbnail size guide](/youtube-thumbnail-size). Keep the bottom-right
corner empty, where YouTube shows the video length. Then put your thumbnail
next to other R.E.P.O. videos in the
[YouTube thumbnail preview](/tools/youtube-thumbnail-preview) and compare
versions with the [thumbnail tester](/tools/youtube-thumbnail-tester). No tool
can predict how many people will click before a video is live. When you
connect your channel, Base.Tube shows the real impressions and click-through
rate of each video.

## What "does he know?" means

The caption is a question about a secret. A character looks calm, or
smirks, and the thumbnail asks whether he knows something. The viewer
cannot tell from the face, so the only way to find out is to watch. That is a
dramatic irony thumbnail: the old theatre trick where the audience knows more than the
person on stage, compressed into three words.

The meme comes from New Rockstars, a channel that breaks down films and
series. In 2022 it posted a video asking whether the Riddler in The Batman
knew Batman's secret identity. The thumbnail paired the character's
unreadable smirk with DOES HE KNOW? in heavy capitals. A Tumblr compilation
of the channel's thumbnails made it go viral, and people on X began posting
it as a reply whenever someone seemed to miss something obvious.

## Why the format works as a thumbnail

A good thumbnail opens a question the title does not close. This one is
literally a question. It also does it with very little: one face, three
words, no arrow or explosion. That restraint makes it stand out next to
louder thumbnails, and it reads well at phone size.

It also flatters the viewer. When the secret is visible in the image, the
viewer feels ahead of the character, and wants to see the moment the
character finds out.

## How to make one

- **Choose the secret.** A trap, a surprise, a clue, a danger. If the video
  has no secret, use another format.
- **Frame the face.** Close-up, unreadable expression: a faint smirk,
  narrowed eyes or a calm stare. A big shocked face kills the question.
- **Show the secret small.** A red circle around it is enough. Keep it on
  the other third so the eye moves from face to secret.
- **Write the caption.** DOES HE KNOW? in heavy condensed capitals, yellow
  or white with a black outline, beside the face.
- **Go dark and cinematic** for theory videos, bright and sunny for pranks.
- **Keep the bottom-right corner empty** for the video length.

## Using it beyond movie theories

The caption travels well. A prank channel shows a smiling dad under a water

## What makes an R6 thumbnail work

Rainbow Six Siege is a game of tension: a wall about to break, a drone in a
vent, a defuser ticking down. The best R6 Siege thumbnail puts the viewer in
that second before something happens. It shows one moment, reads in a second
and is lit well enough to survive a phone screen.

That last point matters more in Siege than in most games. The maps are
indoor, grey and often dark. A screenshot that looks fine full-screen turns
into a dull smudge at feed size. Every rule on this page follows from that.

## One operator, one gadget

Operators are recognised by their gadget as much as by their face. Thermite's
charge, Sledge's hammer, Ash's breaching rounds, Caveira's knife, Jäger's
ADS: put the gadget in the frame and fans know who it is before they read a
word. Make the operator large, cut at the waist or the chest, and point the
gadget or the gaze at the thing that matters.

## Light the scene on purpose

Choose one strong light source and let it tell the story. The white-orange
glow of a breach says attack. A red emergency lamp says danger and clutch. A
cold blue window says a slow, tactical round. Keep the rest of the room dark
or blurred. A Rainbow Six Siege thumbnail with one lit subject beats a fully
lit room every time.

## Show the stakes with the interface

The game's own interface tells fans what is at stake faster than words. A
planted defuser with the timer at five seconds says clutch. A Champion badge
says rank. A drone view says information. Use one of them, large and clean,
and leave the rest of the HUD out.

## Five layouts, one per video type

Most Siege videos fit one of five shapes: **breach**, **clutch**, **rank
climb**, **face cam** or **versus**. The layout recipes above give the
structure of each. Pick the one that matches your video, then change the
operator, the map and the words. Using the same layout across a series helps
returning viewers spot your videos.

## Text: short and square

Three words is the limit. Use capitals, a heavy, square font and a thick black
outline: 1V4, BREACH, COPPER → CHAMP. The words should add something the
image cannot say on its own. Loadouts and attachments belong in the
description, not the thumbnail. To compare heavy display fonts, see the
[YouTube thumbnail fonts guide](/youtube-thumbnail-fonts).

## An R6 thumbnail background that stays behind

The R6 thumbnail background sets the mood and nothing else: a vault, a
basement, a garage, lit by one light. Blur or darken it so the operator and
the text stand out. For more ways to keep a background behind the subject,
see the [YouTube thumbnail backgrounds guide](/youtube-thumbnail-backgrounds).

## Size and checks

Upload at 1280 × 720 pixels, in 16:9. For file size and format limits, see the
[YouTube thumbnail size guide](/youtube-thumbnail-size). Keep the bottom-right
corner empty, where YouTube shows the video length. Then put your thumbnail
next to other Siege videos in the
[YouTube thumbnail preview](/tools/youtube-thumbnail-preview) and compare
versions with the [thumbnail tester](/tools/youtube-thumbnail-tester). No
tool can predict how many people will click before a video is live; when you
connect your channel, Base.Tube shows the real impressions and click-through
rate of each video.

## Where the meme comes from

New Rockstars is a YouTube channel that breaks down films and series:
theories, Easter eggs, endings. For years its thumbnails followed the same
formula: one character from the story, framed close, and a short question in
heavy yellow or white capitals. On its own, each thumbnail was a normal piece
of channel branding.

Then people started using them as reaction images. The first big one was
WHAT WAS HE COOKING?, from a November 2020 video about Thanos in Avengers:
Endgame. A villain with a grave face next to a question about cooking was
funny out of context, and it became a way to say "what was this person
thinking?". In 2022, DOES HE KNOW? (the Riddler from The Batman) went
viral, followed by WHY IS HE LYING? and many more. Today "New Rockstars
thumbnail" is shorthand for the whole format.

## Why the formula works

- **One question, nothing else.** The viewer reads it in under a second.
- **A serious face.** The drama of the character makes the question feel
  urgent, and slightly absurd, which helps both clicks and memes.
- **A promise.** A question in a thumbnail tells the viewer the video holds
  the answer.
- **Consistency.** The same font, colours and framing made the channel's
  videos recognisable in a crowded feed, which is why the format was so easy
  to turn into a meme.

## How to make a thumbnail in this style

- **Write the question first.** Two to four words, ending with a question
  mark, about the character: WHAT WAS HE COOKING?, IS HE GONE?, WHO IS THAT?
- **Create an original character.** Never a film still or an actor. A
  generated character that fits the story's genre works.
- **Frame it close.** Face and shoulders on one side, eyes readable.
- **Grade it.** Teal shadows, orange skin and highlights, a little grain.
- **Write the caption.** Heavy condensed capitals, yellow or white, thick
  black outline, on the other side or along the top.
- **Optional circle.** A red circle around one small background detail for
  Easter-egg videos.
- **Keep the bottom-right corner clear** for the video length.

## Use the question honestly

The format is a promise: the video answers the question. If it does not,
viewers leave quickly, and the format loses its value for your channel. The
question should be the real hook of the video, not a decoration.

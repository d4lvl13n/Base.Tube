## What makes a Marvel Rivals thumbnail work

Marvel Rivals is loud, fast and full of heroes everyone already knows. That
helps your thumbnail: a viewer recognises Hulk, Spider-Man or Groot in a
fraction of a second. The thumbnails that stand out use that recognition and
add one thing: what the hero is doing in this video.

Most viewers see your thumbnail small, on a phone or in a sidebar, next to
other hero shooter videos. Big shapes, one hero and strong colour survive at
that size. A screenshot of a six-versus-six team fight does not.

## One hero, one action

Pick the hero the video is about and make it large. Then show the ability
that matters: Jeff's ultimate swallowing enemies, Groot's walls, Loki's
illusions, Hulk landing in the middle of a team. A hero standing still with
no situation is just character art; a hero in action is a promise.

For versus and team-up videos, use two heroes and no more. Give each one its
own colour and its own side of the frame.

## Comic-book energy

The game borrows from the comics, and so should your thumbnail. Thick
outlines, a slight halftone in the sky, tilted text that reads like a sound
effect. Pick one accent colour: yellow for the ultimate glow and the main
word, red for enemies and damage, purple for magic and portals. Keep the rest
of the frame calm so the accent does the work.

## Show the game's interface

A piece of the real HUD tells fans this is Marvel Rivals and not a comic
cover: the ultimate charge at 100%, a kill feed full of eliminations, a rank
badge, the MVP screen at the end of the match. It also sets the stakes. A
kill feed with six names in it says "team wipe" without any text.

## Five layouts for five kinds of video

Most Marvel Rivals videos fit one of five shapes: **hero spotlight**, **rank
climb**, **face cam**, **versus** or **team-up**. The layout recipes above
give the structure of each. Guides and "broken hero" videos suit the hero
spotlight. Ranked journeys suit the rank climb. Reactions suit the face cam.
Matchups suit versus, and duo strategies suit the team-up.

## Text: three words, tilted

Use one to three words in heavy italic capitals, yellow or white, with a
thick black outline. TOO STRONG, TEAM WIPE, BRONZE → GM: the text adds what
the image cannot say, never the full title. To compare heavy display fonts,
see the [YouTube thumbnail fonts guide](/youtube-thumbnail-fonts).

## A Marvel Rivals thumbnail background

The background is a real map, chosen for the hero: Yggsgard for Thor, Loki
and Hela, Tokyo 2099 for Spider-Man and Venom, Wakanda for Black Panther.
Blur it slightly or darken the edges so the hero stays in front. More ideas
in the [YouTube thumbnail backgrounds guide](/youtube-thumbnail-backgrounds).

## Size and checks

Upload at 1280 × 720 pixels, in 16:9; for file limits see the
[YouTube thumbnail size guide](/youtube-thumbnail-size). Keep the bottom-right
corner clear for the video length. Then put your thumbnail next to other
videos in the [YouTube thumbnail preview](/tools/youtube-thumbnail-preview)
and compare versions with the
[thumbnail tester](/tools/youtube-thumbnail-tester).

No tool can predict how many people will click before a video is live. The
real answer comes from YouTube. When you connect your channel, Base.Tube shows
the real impressions and click-through rate of each video.

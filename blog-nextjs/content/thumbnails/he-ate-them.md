## What the meme is

HE ATE THEM is a three-word horror joke. The picture is a grainy
black-and-white image of something creepy: most often a worn animatronic
rabbit from the Five Nights at Freddy's fan world, sometimes a plush toy, a
creature or a dog. Underneath, in red: HE ATE THEM. There is no context. The
viewer fills in the story, and that is what makes it funny: a horror meme
thumbnail in its purest form.

The image travels as a GIF and a reaction image, tagged with "youtube
thumbnail" on GIF sites. We could not find a documented first post, but its
home is clearly the fan-game and horror-lore side of YouTube, where
black-and-white, high-grain thumbnails with red text have been common for
years. For that wider world, see our
[FNAF thumbnail meme](/thumbnails/fnaf-meme) page.

## Why the look works

- **One colour.** Everything is grey except the caption. The eye has only
  one place to go.
- **Found-footage feel.** Grain, vignette and a slight blur make the image
  feel like a security camera still or an old photo, which is creepier
  than a clean render.
- **Calm subject.** A blank stare suggests the creature is satisfied, and
  that is scarier, and funnier, than a scream.
- **Implied, never shown.** Nothing is actually eaten in the picture. The
  caption does all the work, so the thumbnail stays safe for any audience.

## How to make one

- **Pick the subject.** An original mascot, a toy, a creature or your own
  pet. Never a game's character or a downloaded meme image.
- **Frame it simply.** Centred or slightly off-centre, looking into the
  lens, lots of black around it.
- **Convert to black and white.** Push the contrast, add grain and a dark
  vignette.
- **Add the caption in red.** HE ATE THEM in bold sans-serif, or rough
  hand-painted letters for a creepier feel. Lower case works for a quieter
  joke.
- **Add one clue if you want.** Crumbs on a nose, an empty plate, a pile of
  wrappers.
- **Keep the bottom-right corner clear** for the video length.

## Funny, not gory

The caption is dark, so the image should stay light. No blood, no body
parts, nothing being eaten on screen. The examples above are deliberately
cute or mysterious: a proud dog, a smiling plush bear, an empty arcade. That
keeps the thumbnail within YouTube's rules and makes it shareable. For other
creepy-but-safe looks, see the [cursed thumbnail](/thumbnails/cursed) and
[3 AM thumbnail](/thumbnails/3am) pages.

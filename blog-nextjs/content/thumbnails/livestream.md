## A livestream thumbnail has two lives

Most thumbnails are made once. A livestream thumbnail, like any YouTube live thumbnail, lives twice. First, it
announces the stream: people see it on your channel and in their
subscriptions before you go live and while you are live. Then, once the stream
ends, it becomes the thumbnail of a long replay that has to compete with every
other video.

Plan for both. The upcoming version says what and when. The replay version
says what happened.

## Before and during: what and when

People decide to show up to a stream for one of three reasons: the topic, the
goal or the person. Put one of them in big letters. "Q&A", "NO DEATHS", "24H".
Add the day and time while the stream is scheduled, and a small red LIVE tag
in a top corner. Keep it short: the stream title can carry the details.

## After: sell the replay

The day after, "SAT 8PM" means nothing. Go back into YouTube Studio and
replace the thumbnail with the best moment of the stream: the boss you beat,
the question that made everyone laugh, the goal bar at 100 percent. Remove
the LIVE tag and the date. Your replay now looks like a normal video and can
be recommended like one.

## Five layouts for streams

- **Upcoming**: the streamer, the topic and the day and time.
- **Gaming stream**: the face, the game on a monitor and the goal.
- **Q&A**: the streamer and a few chat bubbles with questions.
- **Marathon**: a big hour count or a goal bar.
- **Live event**: the stage or the screen, a countdown and the LIVE tag.

## Keep a stream look

Regular viewers come back every week. Make your stream thumbnails easy to
spot: the same background, the same neon colours, the same position for your
face and the LIVE tag. Change only the topic, the goal and the time. The
[YouTube thumbnail backgrounds guide](/youtube-thumbnail-backgrounds) has
ideas for backgrounds that stay out of the way.

## Text and corners

Use one to three words in a heavy font with a dark outline; the
[YouTube thumbnail fonts guide](/youtube-thumbnail-fonts) lists free fonts
that read when small. Keep the bottom-right corner clear: YouTube shows the
live badge there during the stream and the length of the replay afterwards.

## Size and the final check

A YouTube live stream uses a normal 16:9 thumbnail; the
[YouTube thumbnail size guide](/youtube-thumbnail-size) has the numbers. Check
both versions, upcoming and replay, next to other videos in the
[YouTube thumbnail preview](/tools/youtube-thumbnail-preview), and compare
options with the [thumbnail tester](/tools/youtube-thumbnail-tester). If you
also run a video podcast, the [podcast thumbnail ideas](/thumbnails/podcast)
page has layouts for episodes.

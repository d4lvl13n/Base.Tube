## What makes an ARC Raiders thumbnail work

ARC Raiders videos all share the same ingredients: raiders in masks, ruined
retro sci-fi maps and giant machines. A viewer scrolling past a dozen of them
needs to see in one second what is different about yours. Was it a solo kill,
a backpack full of rare parts, a betrayal at the extraction?

Many viewers see your thumbnail small: on a phone, in the sidebar, on a TV
across the room. Fine detail like item tooltips and stash grids disappears at
that size. Shapes, size gaps and colour survive. Every rule on this page
follows from that.

## Use the size of the machines

The strongest image in ARC Raiders is a small human next to a huge machine.
Put a raider in the lower part of the frame and let a Bastion, a Rocketeer or
a swarm of Wasps fill the rest. The viewer understands the danger before
reading a single word. For close calls, go the other way: an extreme close-up
of a Leaper's red eye next to a helmet visor says "ambush" on its own.

## One result per raid

Each kind of ARC Raiders video has its own result to show:

- **Machine fights:** the machine huge, the raider small, red eyes and smoke.
- **Extractions:** the overflowing backpack, the countdown, enemies behind.
- **Progression:** a split frame between the free loadout and the full stash.
- **Raider stories:** two raiders and one tension, trust or betrayal.
- **Reactions:** your face next to the moment that made you shout.

Pick one. A thumbnail that tries to show the fight, the loot and the betrayal
at once shows none of them.

## Colours that tell the story

The game already has a colour language: warm sunlight on the ruins, red
lights on the machines, bright loot. Keep red for danger and machine eyes,
yellow for loot and your main word, and let the warm orange and teal sky set
the mood. A sunny frame with one red threat stands out more than a dark,
muddy one.

## Text: short results, thick outlines

Three words is the upper limit. Use capitals, a heavy condensed font and a
thick black outline. The words should say what the image cannot: "SOLO",
"FULL BAG", "BETRAYED", "0 TO RICH". To compare heavy display fonts, see the
[YouTube thumbnail fonts guide](/youtube-thumbnail-fonts).

## An ARC Raiders thumbnail background that stays behind

The map sets the mood. Dam Battlegrounds in daylight says "loot run", the
streets of Buried City say "chase", the Spaceport says "big raid", and the
underground hub of Speranza says "gear and trading". Blur it or darken the
edges so the raider and the machine stay sharp. More ideas in the
[YouTube thumbnail backgrounds guide](/youtube-thumbnail-backgrounds).

## Faces behind masks

Raiders wear helmets and masks, so their faces cannot show fear or joy. Let
the pose do it: crouching behind cover, sprinting with the bag, looking up at
a machine. If your channel shows your face, a face cam reacting next to the
raid adds the emotion. The examples on this page are generated with Base.Tube
and use the game's look and characters; their face cams show invented people.

## Size and checks

Upload at 1280 × 720 pixels, in 16:9. For file size and format limits, see the
[YouTube thumbnail size guide](/youtube-thumbnail-size). Keep the bottom-right
corner empty, where YouTube shows the video length. Then put your thumbnail
next to other ARC Raiders videos in the
[YouTube thumbnail preview](/tools/youtube-thumbnail-preview) and compare
versions with the [thumbnail tester](/tools/youtube-thumbnail-tester). No tool
can predict how many people will click before a video is live. When you
connect your channel, Base.Tube shows the real impressions and click-through
rate of each video.

## What a COD thumbnail has to do

Call of Duty videos share a feed with thousands of clips from the same maps
and the same operators. Viewers who play already know the game. Your
thumbnail does not need to explain it. It needs to show, in one glance, what
is different about this video: the moment, the number or the reaction.

The thumbnails that work now look like the real game, and everything in the
frame is there to make that one moment easier to read.

## What we know about Modern Warfare 4

Checked on 22 October 2026 against Activision's
[official Call of Duty blog](https://www.callofduty.com/blog/2026/05/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-announcement):

- **Release:** Friday 23 October 2026 on PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, PC
  and Nintendo Switch 2. Not on PlayStation 4 or Xbox One.
- **Developer:** development is led by Infinity Ward.
- **Campaign:** set during a North Korean invasion of South Korea. You play
  Private Park, a young South Korean soldier, and the story also follows
  Captain Price. Missions include trench warfare in Korea, close-quarters
  fighting in New York, chases through Paris and night raids in Mumbai.
  Campaign Early Access started on 16 October.
- **Multiplayer:** 12 new core 6v6 maps, plus Kill Block, a training-facility
  arena that rebuilds itself between rounds, and large Ground War battles.
  Hipfire "bloom" (random bullet spread) is removed, and fully levelled
  weapons unlock special Apex Attachments.
- **DMZ:** the extraction mode returns, in a zone called Hajin. You go in
  solo or with a squad and leave with whatever you can carry.

## MW4 launch week: build templates first

In launch week, viewers see the same video idea from several channels on the
same day. The thumbnail is often the only difference they notice. So prepare
one template per video type before the game is out: a reaction, a first
mission, the new maps, a DMZ run. On the day, you only change the word.

Keep campaign thumbnails free of spoilers. Many viewers are still playing the
story, and they skip videos that give the ending away. A mission number or a
place (a snowy trench, a rainy night raid) is enough.

## Realistic gameplay: let the game look real

One style that works well right now is simply a first-person frame
that looks like a photo: rain on the street, light through a window, a
gloved hand on the rifle. The promise is "look how real this is". Keep the
HUD minimal, pick a place with strong light and add at most two words.

## Warzone: one moment, one arrow

A Warzone thumbnail sells a moment: the final circle, a Gulag win, a clutch
at 1 HP, a hiding spot nobody checks. Show it from the player's view or next
to a face cam, and put a red arrow or circle on the detail that makes it
surprising. A small Warzone logo badge in a top corner tells the viewer which
mode this is.

## Text: numbers and short reactions

Three words is the limit. "NUKE", "25 STREAK" and "FINAL CIRCLE" read in under
a second, even at the smallest size. Use capitals, a heavy condensed font and
a thick black outline. Our [YouTube thumbnail fonts guide](/youtube-thumbnail-fonts)
lists free fonts that hold up at small sizes. Do not repeat the video title.

## One accent colour

Give one colour a job: orange for the gas wall and nukes, gold for counts,
night-vision green for MW4 night raids and DMZ smoke, red for arrows and
enemies. Two accents compete; one accent leads the eye. Keep the area behind
the text calm; the
[YouTube thumbnail backgrounds guide](/youtube-thumbnail-backgrounds) shows
how.

## Size, format and the final check

Export at 1280 × 720 pixels in a 16:9 frame; the
[YouTube thumbnail size guide](/youtube-thumbnail-size) has the current file
limits. Keep the bottom-right corner clear, because YouTube puts the video
length there. Then put your thumbnail next to real videos in the
[YouTube thumbnail preview](/tools/youtube-thumbnail-preview) and compare
two versions with the [thumbnail tester](/tools/youtube-thumbnail-tester).
No tool can tell you in advance how many people will click; the real answer
comes from how the video performs once it is live.

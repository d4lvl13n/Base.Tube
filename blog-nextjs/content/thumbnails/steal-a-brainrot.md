## What a Steal a Brainrot thumbnail has to do

Steal a Brainrot videos all share the same setting: a row of player bases,
a conveyor full of strange brainrots, Roblox avatars running in every
direction. A viewer scrolling through results sees dozens of them side by
side. The setting alone does not make anyone click.

What makes them click is the story of this one video: the Secret brainrot
that just spawned, the base you emptied in ten seconds, the friend who
betrayed you. A Roblox Steal a Brainrot thumbnail has one job: show that
story in a single look, at phone size.

## The brainrot is the star

Fans know the brainrots by heart. Tralalero Tralala, Tung Tung Tung Sahur,
Brr Brr Patapim: each one has a strong, silly shape that reads even when the
thumbnail is tiny. Make the brainrot the biggest object in the frame, bigger
than the avatars, and let it look straight at the viewer or be carried away
over someone's head.

## Use the rarity colours players already read

The game teaches players to read rarity by colour. Gold says Brainrot God,
pink says Mythic, black and white with a glow says Secret. Use one of these as
the accent of your thumbnail and keep the rest of the frame calm. A gold glow
behind a brainrot tells the viewer "this is valuable" before they read a word.
Keep red for alarms, laser doors and arrows.

## Show the heist, not just the base

A base full of brainrots is nice, but a base being robbed is a story. Pick
the moment with movement and stakes:

- **The steal:** an avatar sprinting out with the brainrot, the owner chasing.
- **The rare spawn:** one huge brainrot on the conveyor, everyone staring.
- **The glow-up:** an empty base next to a full one, an arrow between them.
- **The defence:** one player at the laser door, a line of thieves outside.
- **The reaction:** your face next to the moment it all went wrong.

## Text: name the result

Three words is the limit. Use capitals, a heavy rounded font and a thick
black outline. The words should add something the image cannot say on its
own: STOLEN, SECRET, NOOB → RICH, 24 HOURS. Avoid prices and long numbers;
they turn into noise when the thumbnail is small. To compare heavy display
fonts, see the [YouTube thumbnail fonts guide](/youtube-thumbnail-fonts).

## A Steal a Brainrot thumbnail background that stays behind

The Steal a Brainrot thumbnail background sets the mood and nothing else. A
bright sky over the bases says "fun heist". A dark night with one glow says
"rare spawn". Whatever you choose, blur it or darken it so the brainrot and
the avatar stand out. For more ways to keep a background behind the subject,
see the [YouTube thumbnail backgrounds guide](/youtube-thumbnail-backgrounds).

## Faces and avatars

Roblox faces are simple, so the pose does the work: running, arms up, hands
on the head. A face cam of you reacting next to the game adds the real
emotion. The examples on this page are generated with Base.Tube and use the
game's own brainrots and bases; their face cams show invented people.

## Size and checks

Upload at 1280 × 720 pixels, in 16:9. For file size and format limits, see the
[YouTube thumbnail size guide](/youtube-thumbnail-size). Keep the bottom-right
corner empty, where YouTube shows the video length. Then put your thumbnail
next to other videos in the
[YouTube thumbnail preview](/tools/youtube-thumbnail-preview) and compare
versions with the [thumbnail tester](/tools/youtube-thumbnail-tester). No tool
can predict how many people will click before a video is live; when you
connect your channel, Base.Tube shows the real impressions and click-through
rate of each video.

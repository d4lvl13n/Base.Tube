## What reaction images do in a thumbnail

A reaction video thumbnail sells two things at once: the content and the person
watching it. YouTube thumbnail reaction images do the same in one frame. A
cut-out face, showing one strong emotion, sits on top of the thing being
reacted to. The viewer sees the clip, the product or the game, and instantly
knows how the creator felt about it.

When people search for thumbnail reaction images, they usually want a ready
face to drop into their own thumbnail. The best reaction face for a
thumbnail is your own, because it builds recognition every time a viewer sees
it. This page shows how to make a set.

## Build your own reaction set

Reaction channels keep a library of their own faces and reuse it. Making one
takes an hour:

- **Light:** face a window or a soft light, so the face is evenly lit.
- **Background:** a plain wall makes cut-outs easy.
- **Emotions:** shock, laughter, disgust, confusion, disbelief, cringe,
  excitement, fear. Ten to fifteen in total.
- **Several shots each:** keep the one where the eyes and mouth agree.
- **Cut out once:** save each face as a transparent PNG with a thin white
  edge and a soft shadow.

If you run a faceless channel, generate one consistent synthetic character
and give it the same set of emotions. The Base.Tube Studio can generate an
original character in each expression.

## Five layouts that cover most videos

- **Face and screen** for reactions to clips, trailers and music.
- **Two reactions** for duos with opposite opinions.
- **Product reaction** for taste tests, unboxings and reviews.
- **Phone reaction** for reading comments, DMs and posts.
- **Reaction grid** for group reactions and compilations.

Whatever the layout, keep one thing true: the eyes or an arrow point at the
content. A face looking into empty space breaks the link.

## Making it readable

The face should take up a third of the frame or more. At phone size, small
faces turn into dots and the emotion disappears. Blur or darken the content
slightly so the face stays the hero, and keep any text to three words. For
the history of the exaggerated expression itself, see our page on the
[YouTube thumbnail face](/thumbnails/youtube-face); for a speech-bubble
twist, see [zamn thumbnails](/thumbnails/zamn).

## Respect the content you react to

A reaction video uses someone else's work as material. Your face and
commentary should be the main subject of the thumbnail, not a full frame of
the original. Never copy the original video's thumbnail, and never use
another creator's face as a reaction image.

## Test before you upload

Check that the emotion reads at feed size in the
[YouTube thumbnail preview](/tools/youtube-thumbnail-preview), and compare
two faces for the same video with the
[thumbnail tester](/tools/youtube-thumbnail-tester). Export at 1280 × 720;
the [YouTube thumbnail size guide](/youtube-thumbnail-size) has the limits.

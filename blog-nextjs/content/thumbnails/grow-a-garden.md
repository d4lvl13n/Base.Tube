## What makes a Grow a Garden thumbnail work

Grow a Garden videos all share the same picture: Roblox avatars, green
plots, rows of plants. In a feed full of them, that picture says nothing new.
Your thumbnail has to show the one thing that makes this video worth a
click: a rarer seed, a bigger mutation, a better pet, a garden that changed
completely.

Most viewers of Grow a Garden videos watch on a phone, often young players
checking what is new between sessions. At that size, a whole garden turns into
green noise. One huge plant, one glowing pet and one bold word survive. Every
rule on this page follows from that.

## Make the rare thing huge

The game is about wanting things: the rare seed in the shop, the mutation on
your fruit, the pet in the egg. Show that thing bigger than it is in the game.
A fruit larger than the avatar holding it, an egg filling half the frame,
light pouring out of it. The size alone tells the viewer it is special.

## Glow means rare

The game already uses colour to say how rare something is. Gold and rainbow
mutations, glowing seeds, eggs that shine. Use the same language: gold glow
for a gold mutation, rainbow colours for a rainbow one, red for a Blood Moon.
Keep green as a background colour only, because every Grow a Garden thumbnail
is already full of it.

## Five layouts, one per video type

Most Grow a Garden videos fit one of five shapes:

- **Rare find:** one giant plant or fruit, glowing with its mutation.
- **Glow-up:** an empty plot next to a full garden, day 1 next to day 30.
- **Egg hatch:** an egg cracking open on a rare pet.
- **Face cam:** your reaction to a restock, a mutation or a trade.
- **Event night:** the garden under a special sky, like a Blood Moon.

Pick the layout that matches the promise of your video. Keeping the same
layout across a series helps returning viewers spot your videos.

## Roblox faces and poses

Roblox avatars have simple faces, so the pose and the classic shocked face do
the work: arms up, pointing at the fruit, hugging a pet. If your channel shows
your face, a face cam reacting next to the garden adds real excitement. The
examples on this page are generated with Base.Tube and use the game's look;
their face cams show invented people.

## Text: one rarity word

Three words is the limit, and one word is often better: "RAINBOW", "GOLD",
"FINALLY", "IN STOCK". Use a heavy rounded font with a thick black outline,
gold or white. See the [YouTube thumbnail fonts guide](/youtube-thumbnail-fonts)
for options.

## A Grow a Garden thumbnail background

Your own plots in daylight are the default. A night sky or a red Blood Moon
suits events. Blur everything behind the subject so the plant or the pet stays
sharp. More ideas in the
[YouTube thumbnail backgrounds guide](/youtube-thumbnail-backgrounds).

## Size and checks

Upload at 1280 × 720 pixels, in 16:9. For file size and format limits, see the
[YouTube thumbnail size guide](/youtube-thumbnail-size). Keep the bottom-right
corner empty, where YouTube shows the video length. Then put your thumbnail
next to other Grow a Garden videos in the
[YouTube thumbnail preview](/tools/youtube-thumbnail-preview) and compare
versions with the [thumbnail tester](/tools/youtube-thumbnail-tester). No tool
can predict how many people will click before a video is live. When you
connect your channel, Base.Tube shows the real impressions and click-through
rate of each video.

## What a football thumbnail has to show

American football is a game of short, explosive plays. Each one lasts a few
seconds and has a single peak: the ball arriving in the hands, the throw
leaving the arm, the runner breaking the last tackle. A football YouTube
thumbnail should freeze that peak and add one or two words. Everything else,
the crowd, the scoreboard, the other twenty players, stays in the background.

Every example on this page uses invented players in plain, invented uniforms
such as maroon and silver, orange or teal, with no logos and no real numbers.
The image can feel like a prime-time game without copying a team or a player.

## Helmets change the rules

In most sports, a face sells the thumbnail. In football, the face is often
behind a facemask. So the body has to carry the emotion: full extension on a
catch, arms raised in the end zone, a stiff arm, a quarterback leaning into a
throw. When you need a face, use a sideline moment with the helmet off, or a
presenter for reactions and fantasy videos.

## Five layouts for football videos

- **Highlight**: one play at its peak, under stadium lights.
- **Film breakdown**: a high view of the field with routes and one circle.
- **Fantasy**: a presenter, a verdict and START and SIT labels.
- **Training**: a sprint start, a drill or a stopwatch, in daylight.
- **Game day**: two invented uniforms split by a VS badge, for previews.

Keep one layout per series so returning viewers spot your uploads quickly.

## Film breakdowns: show where to look

Analysis viewers want the secret of the play. Freeze the field at the snap or
the throw, draw the routes in white, and circle the one player who matters in
yellow. Darken the rest if needed. If you draw more than four or five lines,
the play disappears.

## Fantasy football: one verdict

Fantasy videos are decisions: who to start, who to sit, who to pick up. The
thumbnail should show the type of decision, not all the names. A presenter
with a confident or worried face, a green START and a red SIT label, or a
short "TOP 3" list, is enough. Keep player names in the title.

## Colour and text

The field gives you green and white lines. A night sky gives you navy. Use
yellow for marks and key words, red for warnings, and invent uniform colours
that pop against green. Text should be one to three words in a heavy
condensed font; the [YouTube thumbnail fonts guide](/youtube-thumbnail-fonts)
lists free ones that hold up at small sizes.

## Size and the final check

Export at 1280 × 720 pixels in 16:9; the
[YouTube thumbnail size guide](/youtube-thumbnail-size) has the current
limits. Keep the bottom-right corner clear for the video length. Then put your
thumbnail next to other football videos in the
[YouTube thumbnail preview](/tools/youtube-thumbnail-preview) and compare two
versions with the [thumbnail tester](/tools/youtube-thumbnail-tester).

## What the format is

Death Battle is a web series built on one question: who would win? Two
characters from different universes are matched up, their abilities are
analysed, and an animated fight settles it. Its thumbnails answer the
"who" before the viewer even reads the title: two fighters, face to face,
each on their own colour, with a large VS between them.

The VS thumbnail is older than the show, from fighting-game select screens
to boxing posters. But many people now search for a Death Battle thumbnail
template when they want that matchup look for their own videos.

## Why the VS layout works

- **Instant story.** Two sides, one winner. The viewer understands the
  premise in a fraction of a second.
- **Built-in question.** A matchup always raises "who wins?", and the only
  way to find out is to watch.
- **Clear colour code.** Red against blue, fire against ice: the two halves
  are two camps, even at phone size.
- **Symmetry.** Two fighters of equal size on a central axis feel balanced
  and dramatic, like a poster.

## How to build a versus thumbnail

- **Split the frame.** A diagonal line from top to bottom feels more
  dynamic than a straight vertical one. Add sparks or lightning along it.
- **Pick two contrasting colours.** One per side, saturated.
- **Place the fighters.** One per half, facing the centre, about the same
  size, with their signature weapon or power visible.
- **Add the VS.** Huge, centred on the split line, metallic gold or white
  with a black outline.
- **Names are optional.** If you add them, keep them short and at the top.
- **Keep the bottom-right corner clear** for the video length.

## Beyond fighters

The versus thumbnail works for any comparison. A cheap kettle against an
expensive one. A cat against a robot vacuum. Two phones, two cities, two
diets. Lighting everyday objects like fighters, with glow and smoke, is part
of the joke and still tells the viewer exactly what the video compares. For
a price-led take on the same idea, see our
[MrBeast-style thumbnails](/thumbnails/mrbeast).

## Original fighters

The examples here use invented fighters only. Matchup channels that create
their own recurring characters get two benefits: no question about who owns
the images, and thumbnails nobody else can copy. If your matchups are about
existing characters, avoid the show's logo and title card and build your
own branding.

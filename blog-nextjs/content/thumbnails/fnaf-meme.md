## Why FNAF thumbnails became a meme

Five Nights at Freddy's arrived in 2014 and quickly became one of the most
watched games on YouTube. Players filmed themselves screaming at jumpscares,
and theory channels dissected every shadow on every camera. Thousands of
videos meant thousands of Five Nights at Freddy's thumbnails, and they converged on the same moves:
a dark office, two glowing eyes in a doorway, a screaming face in a
headset, red arrows pointing at a smudge, and words like LEAKED, SECRET or
IT'S REAL followed by "?!".

Repeated that often, the formula became a joke. People now share
deliberately absurd fnaf clickbait thumbnail parodies, and there is even a
fan-made game that lets you invent fake FNAF titles and thumbnails. When
someone searches for the FNAF thumbnail meme, they usually want either that
parody or the formula behind it.

## The four meme moves

### Glowing eyes

Two small white dots in a black doorway. It is the most effective move in
the set because it shows almost nothing. The viewer's imagination fills in
the rest. Keep the eyes sharp and the rest of the frame very dark.

### The jumpscare face

A player with a headset, mouth wide open, falling back from the screen. It
sells the video as a reaction. Use your own face, or an original character,
never another creator's.

### Red arrows and LEAKED?!

The parody move. A night-vision camera feed, a vague shadow, three red
arrows and a huge LEAKED?! It is funny precisely because it promises too
much. Use it as an obvious joke, not as a real claim.

### The theory board

A cork board, polaroids, red string and a serious face with a hand on the
chin. It tells the viewer this is a lore video.

## How to make your own

- **Pick one move.** Eyes, scream, arrows or board, not all four.
- **Use an original animatronic.** An animatronic thumbnail lives or dies on the mascot. An invented bear, fox, bunny or chicken
  mascot reads instantly as "FNAF-like" without copying anything.
- **Light one thing.** Eyes, a flashlight beam or a monitor glow, and keep
  everything else dark.
- **Write one to three words** in heavy yellow or red capitals with a black
  outline.
- **Keep the bottom-right corner clear** for the video length.

## Keep it honest and safe

LEAKED, REAL and SECRET are fine as jokes everyone recognises. On a video
that is not a joke, they are a promise. If nothing leaks, viewers leave. The
games also have a large young audience, so keep the scares suggestive:
glowing eyes, not gore.

dos:
  - Sketch at least six rough ideas before making the real thumbnail.
  - Draw your sketches in 16:9 boxes, small, so you judge them at feed size.
  - Mark where the text and the bottom-right timestamp will go.
  - For a drawn look, keep one colour accent and bold lines.
  - Leave the bottom-right corner clear for the video length.
donts:
  - Don't polish your first idea; the first sketch is rarely the best.
  - Don't add detail to a planning sketch; shapes and placement are enough.
  - Don't use thin pencil lines alone in a final thumbnail; they vanish when small.
  - Don't trace someone else's artwork or thumbnail.

## What a thumbnail sketch is

A thumbnail sketch is a small, quick, rough drawing that plans a
composition. The name comes from its size: about as small as a thumbnail.
It shows big shapes and placement, where the subject sits, where the empty
space is, where text could go, and nothing else. No detail, no shading
beyond a few tones.

Artists, illustrators, graphic designers, animators and comic artists use
thumbnail sketches all the time. A page of ten of them takes a few minutes,
and comparing them side by side shows which composition works before any
real time is spent. That is the purpose of a thumbnail sketch: explore many
ideas fast, then commit to the best one.

## Why YouTube creators should sketch first

A YouTube thumbnail is seen small, on a phone, in a grid of other images.
A thumbnail drawing that is tiny and rough is a surprisingly good preview of
that experience. If an idea is not clear as a ten-second sketch in a small
box, it will not be clear as a finished image either.

Sketching also stops you from polishing the first idea that comes to mind.
Six quick thumbnail sketches almost always include a stronger version: a
closer crop, a different angle, the object bigger, the text on the other
side.

## How to sketch a thumbnail in five minutes

- **Draw six to ten 16:9 boxes** on one page, small, about the size of a
  phone thumbnail.
- **One idea per box.** Circle for a face, a simple shape for the object,
  lines for text.
- **Vary one thing each time.** Crop, angle, size of the subject, side of
  the text.
- **Shade the bottom-right corner** in every box. YouTube puts the video
  length there.
- **Step back and pick.** The clearest box from arm's length wins.
- **Build it.** Recreate the winning sketch in your editor, or describe it
  in the Base.Tube Studio and generate it.

## The hand-drawn look as a style

A sketch thumbnail can also be the final image. Pencil drawings, marker
doodles on notebook paper and whiteboard-style drawings stand out in a feed
full of photos, and they signal a handmade, human video. They suit art and
design channels, study tips, explainers and storytelling.

To keep a drawn thumbnail readable:

- **Bold lines.** Thin pencil vanishes at small size; go over the main
  outlines in marker.
- **One colour accent.** A highlighter yellow object on grey graphite draws
  the eye instantly.
- **Hand-lettered text, big.** One to three words.
- **Light paper background.** Cream or white, so the lines contrast.

NFT Content Pass Revenue Simulation for Base.Tube

Published on September 18, 2024 by CG INTERNATIONAL CONSULTING

Executive Summary

This report provides a comprehensive simulation of the revenue potential for Base.Tube's NFT Content Pass feature. The simulation incorporates key variables such as user retention, price elasticity, and scenario planning to project earnings for both creators and Base.Tube over a one-year period. By merging initial projections with advanced considerations, we aim to offer actionable insights to optimize pricing strategies, support creators, and enhance platform profitability.

1. Introduction

Base.Tube is pioneering a unique approach in the creator economy with its NFT Content Passes, offering creators new monetization avenues and providing fans with exclusive content access. To understand the financial implications of this feature, we have conducted a detailed simulation that integrates initial projections with advanced considerations such as user retention and price elasticity.

2. Objectives

  • Estimate Revenue Generation: Calculate potential earnings for creators and Base.Tube from initial sales and secondary market activities.
  • Incorporate Market Dynamics: Factor in user retention rates, price elasticity, and varying engagement levels.
  • Provide Strategic Insights: Offer recommendations to optimize pricing strategies and support sustainable growth.

3. Methodology

Our simulation combines quantitative analysis with scenario planning to project revenues over a one-year period. We segmented creators into tiers based on their follower counts, applied bonding curve pricing models, and incorporated user behavior factors such as churn rates and price sensitivity.

4. Assumptions and Data Inputs

4.1. Creator Segmentation

Total Creators: 5,000

  • Top Creators (10%): 500 creators
  • Mid-Level Creators (40%): 2,000 creators
  • Emerging Creators (50%): 2,500 creators

4.2. NFT Content Pass Parameters

  • Passes per Creator: Up to 1,000
  • Starting Price: $5
  • Maximum Price Cap: $250
  • Bonding Curve: Linear increase of $0.245 per pass

4.3. Fees and Royalties

  • Creator's Revenue Share (Initial Sale): 90%
  • Base.Tube's Fee (Initial Sale): 10%
  • Creator's Royalty (Resale): 5%
  • Base.Tube's Fee (Resale): 2.5%

4.4. User Base Dynamics

  • Starting Active Users: 100,000
  • Monthly User Growth Rate: Varies by scenario
  • Monthly Churn Rate: Varies by scenario
  • Engagement Rates:
    • Top Creators: 5% - 10%
    • Mid-Level Creators: 3% - 7%
    • Emerging Creators: 1% - 5%

4.5. Price Elasticity of Demand (PED)

  • Top Creators: PED = -0.5
  • Mid-Level Creators: PED = -1.0
  • Emerging Creators: PED = -1.5

4.6. Secondary Market Activity

  • Percentage of Passes Resold:
    • Top Creators: 50%
    • Mid-Level Creators: 30%
    • Emerging Creators: 10%
  • Average Resale Price Increase:
    • Top Creators: 120% of original price
    • Mid-Level Creators: 110%
    • Emerging Creators: 100%

5. Simulation Details

5.1. Creator Segmentation

We categorized creators based on their follower counts to reflect different levels of influence and audience engagement:

  • Top Creators: 10,000 - 25,000 followers
  • Mid-Level Creators: 2,500 - 10,000 followers
  • Emerging Creators: 500 - 2,500 followers

5.2. Pricing Models

Linear Bonding Curve:

Price increases linearly with each pass sold. The price increase per pass is calculated as:

Price Increase per Pass = (Maximum Price - Starting Price) / (Total Passes - 1)

Price Increase per Pass = ($250 - $5) / (1,000 - 1) ≈ $0.245 per pass

5.3. User Base Dynamics

We calculated the active user base each month, accounting for new user acquisition and churn:

Active Users Month n = Active Usersn-1 + (Active Usersn-1 × Growth Rate) - (Active Usersn-1 × Churn Rate)

5.4. Revenue Calculations

For each creator tier, we calculated:

  • Initial Sales Revenue: Total revenue from selling NFT Content Passes.
  • Creators' Earnings: 90% of initial sales revenue.
  • Base.Tube's Earnings: 10% of initial sales revenue.
  • Secondary Market Revenue: Royalties and fees from resales.

  • Emerging Creators: Most affected by price increases, leading to lower sales volumes.

7.3. User Retention

  • Churn Rates: Significantly impact the active user base and potential market size.
  • User Growth: Higher growth rates amplify revenue potential.

8. Recommendations

8.1. Support Emerging Creators

  • Offer promotional incentives to reduce price sensitivity.
  • Provide marketing assistance to help them reach wider audiences.

8.2. Enhance User Retention

  • Implement engagement features to increase platform stickiness.
  • Introduce loyalty programs to reward long-term users and reduce churn rates.

8.3. Optimize Pricing Strategies

  • Consider flexible pricing by adjusting bonding curve parameters based on creator tier and market response.
  • Explore dynamic pricing models to encourage early purchases.

8.4. Educate Users

  • Provide tutorials to demystify NFTs and blockchain technology.
  • Ensure transparent communication about pricing models and benefits.

9. Conclusion

The NFT Content Pass feature presents a lucrative opportunity for both creators and Base.Tube. By strategically addressing factors such as user retention, price elasticity, and varying market conditions, Base.Tube can optimize revenue generation and support sustainable growth. Implementing the recommendations outlined will enhance the platform's appeal to creators and users alike, positioning Base.Tube as a leader in the evolving creator economy.

Appendix C: Price Elasticity Impact

This appendix illustrates how price changes affect the quantity demanded for each creator tier based on their Price Elasticity of Demand (PED).

1. Understanding Price Elasticity

Price Elasticity of Demand (PED):

PED = (% Change in Quantity Demanded) / (% Change in Price)

  • PED = -0.5: For every 1% increase in price, quantity demanded decreases by 0.5%.
  • PED = -1.0: For every 1% increase in price, quantity demanded decreases by 1%.
  • PED = -1.5: For every 1% increase in price, quantity demanded decreases by 1.5%.

2. Calculations for Mid-Level Creators

Starting Price: $5.00

Final Price: $50.86

Percentage Change in Price:

((Final Price - Starting Price) / Starting Price) × 100% = ((\$50.86 - \$5.00) / \$5.00) × 100% = 917.2%

Applying PED (-1.0):

% Change in Quantity Demanded = -1.0 × 917.2% = -917.2%

Such a large decrease indicates that demand would drop significantly at higher prices, which is why we assume an average decrease in demand of 40% for the simulation.

3. Impact on Passes Sold

Initial Potential Buyers: 312.5 fans

Adjusted Buyers: 312.5 × (1 - 40%) = 187.5 fans

Passes Sold per Creator: 188 passes

4. Similar Calculations for Other Creator Tiers

Assumed Decrease in Demand: 25%

Adjusted Buyers: 1,225 × (1 - 25%) = 918.75 fans (Capped at 1,000 passes)

Appendix D: Glossary of Terms

  • NFT (Non-Fungible Token): A unique digital asset representing ownership of a specific item or content.
  • Bonding Curve: A mathematical function defining the relationship between price and supply of a token or asset.
  • Price Elasticity of Demand (PED): An economic measure of how the quantity demanded responds to a change in price.
  • Churn Rate: The percentage of users who stop using a service during a specific time period.
  • Engagement Rate: The percentage of an audience that interacts with content or takes a desired action.

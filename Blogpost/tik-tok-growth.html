How to Make Money on TikTok: A Step-by-Step Guide to Monetising Your Account

Published on September 6, 2024 by CryptoDBS

With over 1 billion active users monthly, TikTok has evolved from a fun app for lip-sync videos to a powerful platform for creators to earn money. From beauty tips to educational content, TikTok offers diverse ways to monetize your creativity and reach a global audience. In this guide, we'll explore both TikTok's native monetization features and alternative income streams, with real-life examples, specific strategies for different niches, and advanced tips for optimizing your growth.

What is TikTok?

TikTok is a short-form video platform that enables creators to connect with global audiences through engaging, often viral, videos. Although it started as a lip-syncing app (originally called Musical.ly), it has since transformed into a space for education, entertainment, politics, and more. What makes TikTok special is its community-driven nature and short-form content that quickly captivates audiences.

How Creators Are Monetizing TikTok: Real-Life Examples

Let's start by looking at some creators who have successfully monetized their TikTok accounts:

  • Charli D'Amelio (Entertainment/Niche: Dance): One of TikTok's biggest success stories, Charli D'Amelio grew her following through engaging dance videos and later capitalized on brand sponsorships, partnerships, and her own merchandise. She has collaborated with brands like Dunkin' Donuts and launched her own clothing line, which has expanded her revenue beyond TikTok's native tools.
  • Nabela Noor (Lifestyle/Body Positivity): Nabela Noor uses TikTok to promote self-love and body positivity. She has leveraged her growing platform to launch her own brand, Zeba, which offers apparel that champions inclusivity. Her authenticity has attracted sponsorships from beauty and lifestyle brands.
  • Jeremy Sry (Fitness/Health): Jeremy Sry grew his TikTok audience through fitness and health-related content. With over a million followers, he monetizes through partnerships with fitness brands, affiliate marketing, and direct sponsorships.

Native TikTok Monetization Features

TikTok offers a variety of in-app monetization methods, including:

  • TikTok Creator Fund: Creators who meet the eligibility criteria (18+, 10,000+ followers, 100,000+ views in the last 30 days) can apply for the Creator Fund. TikTok pays creators based on views, though payments vary depending on engagement, region, and ad revenue generated from the video.
  • TikTok Creator Next: This initiative adds features like virtual gifts during livestreams, video tips, and access to TikTok Creator Marketplace, where creators can partner with brands.
  • Live Gifting and Virtual Items: During TikTok livestreams, followers can send virtual gifts that convert into monetary earnings.
  • TikTok Creator Marketplace: This feature helps creators collaborate with brands. Creators can proactively search for and partner with brands that align with their content and audience.

Expanding Your Income: Alternative Revenue Streams

While TikTok's native tools are helpful, many successful creators earn additional income through external sources. Here's how you can diversify your revenue streams:

  • Affiliate Marketing: By promoting products through affiliate links (on TikTok or external platforms), creators earn commissions on sales. For example, beauty influencers can link to skincare products in their bio.
  • Merchandise: Selling branded merchandise is a great way to build your brand while generating income. Platforms like Teespring or Printify allow creators to design, market, and sell without managing inventory.
  • Patreon or Memberships: For creators offering premium content, platforms like Patreon allow fans to support them directly through monthly memberships.
  • Paid Content and Courses: TikTok can be a great platform to promote online courses. For example, an English tutor could offer free mini-lessons on TikTok, then direct followers to paid courses on platforms like Udemy.

Regional Differences in Monetization

TikTok's monetization options and eligibility requirements vary by region. While creators in major markets have greater access to monetization tools, those in smaller or emerging markets can still grow by focusing on:

  • Localized Content: Tailor your content to regional trends and interests. Creators from smaller markets can find success by appealing to niche audiences.
  • Cross-Platform Growth: If TikTok's monetization features aren't available in your region, promote your content on platforms like YouTube, Instagram, or Twitch to diversify your reach and income.
  • Affiliate Marketing and Sponsorships: Creators can seek local sponsorships or engage in affiliate marketing, which doesn't require TikTok's internal tools.

Strategies for Different Types of Creators

The strategies for monetizing TikTok vary by niche. Here are a few tailored approaches:

  • Beauty/Fashion Influencers: Partner with makeup, skincare, or clothing brands through TikTok's Creator Marketplace or affiliate marketing. Use high-quality visuals and tutorials to promote products.
  • Educational Creators: Post short, engaging educational videos, and drive traffic to more in-depth content like online courses or premium tutorials.
  • Entertainment Creators: Collaborate with other TikTok influencers to expand your reach. Use TikTok Live to engage your followers and earn via virtual gifts.

Accelerating Growth with TikTok Ads

One overlooked but powerful tool for growth on TikTok is TikTok Ads Manager. By running targeted ads, you can promote your content, drive engagement, and boost your following. Here's how to use TikTok's advertising platform effectively:

  • Targeted Advertising: TikTok Ads Manager allows you to target specific demographics or interests, making it easier to reach your ideal audience.
  • In-Feed Ads: Promote your TikTok videos directly in users' "For You" feeds, mimicking organic content to increase engagement.
  • Branded Hashtag Challenges: Run ads that promote hashtag challenges, encouraging your audience to participate and increasing visibility.

Advanced Analytics for Maximizing Earnings

As you grow, it's crucial to keep refining your content strategy. TikTok Analytics offers detailed insights into your audience, video performance, and engagement rates. Here are some advanced tips for using data to improve your earnings:

  • Track Audience Demographics: Regularly check your audience's age, gender, and location to ensure your content aligns with their interests.
  • Measure Engagement Rates: High engagement rates lead to better visibility. Videos with more likes, comments, and shares tend to perform better in TikTok's algorithm.
  • Identify Peak Posting Times: Use analytics to find when your audience is most active and schedule your posts during these high-traffic windows.
"Monetizing your TikTok account in 2024 requires a combination of authentic content, strategic partnerships, and diversifying your income streams. Focus on engagement and creativity, and you can turn your TikTok account into a lucrative business."

Conclusion: Growing and Monetizing on TikTok in 2024

Monetizing your TikTok account in 2024 requires a combination of authentic content, strategic partnerships, and diversifying your income streams. While TikTok's native tools like the Creator Fund and Creator Next are great starting points, the key to long-term success is expanding beyond the platform through affiliate marketing, merchandise, and external platforms like Patreon.

Tailor your strategies based on your niche, region, and audience, and don't shy away from leveraging TikTok Ads Manager to accelerate your growth. With persistence, creativity, and a focus on engagement, you can turn your TikTok account into a lucrative business while staying true to your brand and values. Happy creating!

Base.Tube Moments & Ranking System

Last Updated: March 15, 2024 by Base.Tube Team

Welcome to Base.Tube's Moments & Ranking System—a unique way to celebrate your engagement on our platform. Whether you're here to watch or create, every interaction counts and brings you closer to unlocking exclusive achievements and rewards. Read on to learn how it works and what benefits you can earn.

What Are Base.Tube Moments?

Base.Tube Moments are achievement-based NFTs that recognize your activity and contributions on our platform. When you hit a milestone, you'll have the opportunity to claim a unique NFT that represents your rank for the current season. These Moments aren't just collectibles—they're a symbol of your journey and a passport to exclusive end-of-season rewards.

How Does the Ranking System Work?

Our ranking system is designed to be clear, engaging, and inclusive for everyone:

Rank-Based Progression

  • Rising Voyager: 0 – 49 points
  • Engaged Contributor: 50 – 249 points
  • Community Builder: 250 – 499 points
  • Platform Pioneer: 500 – 1,249 points
  • Innovative Icon: 1,250 – 2,499 points
  • Base.Tube Legend: 2,500 points and above
